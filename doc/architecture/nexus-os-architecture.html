NEXUS OS · architecture as built, 18 September 2026

An AI business operating system for the GCC — a Next.js BFF over a single FastAPI process over one Neon Postgres with forced row-level security. Seven directors read one Company Brain; every number is computed by deterministic Python and the model is only ever allowed to phrase it. Read out of the repository, not out of its documents.
