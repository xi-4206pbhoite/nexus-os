BANK FACILITY LETTER

Facility. An overdraft facility of 75,000 OMR is made available on the current
account.

Interest. Interest is charged at 6.5 percent per annum on the daily drawn
balance, debited quarterly.

Arrangement fee. An arrangement fee of 750 OMR is payable on acceptance and is
non-refundable.

Review. The facility is repayable on demand and is reviewed annually on
31 December.

Covenants. The borrower shall provide audited accounts within 120 days of each
financial year end, and management accounts quarterly within 30 days of each
quarter end.

Security. The facility is secured by a corporate guarantee. No personal
guarantee is required.
