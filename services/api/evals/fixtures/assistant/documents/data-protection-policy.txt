DATA PROTECTION POLICY — Meridian Trading LLC

Scope. This policy covers personal data held about employees, customers and
suppliers, in any format.

Retention. Employee records are retained for 7 years after the end of
employment. Customer transaction records are retained for 10 years. Marketing
contact data is deleted after 24 months of inactivity.

Access requests. An individual may request a copy of the personal data held
about them. Requests are answered within 30 days at no charge.

Breach notification. A suspected data breach must be reported to the data
protection officer within 24 hours of discovery.

Third parties. Personal data is shared with third parties only under a written
agreement that imposes equivalent obligations.
