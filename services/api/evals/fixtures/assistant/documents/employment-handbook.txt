EMPLOYMENT HANDBOOK — Meridian Trading LLC

Working hours. The standard working week is 45 hours, Sunday to Thursday.
During Ramadan the working day is reduced to 6 hours for all staff.

Annual leave. Employees accrue 30 calendar days of annual leave per completed
year of service. Leave accrues monthly at 2.5 days per month. Unused leave may
be carried forward to a maximum of 15 days.

Probation. New employees serve a probation period of 3 months, extendable once
by a further 3 months with written notice.

Sick leave. Up to 10 weeks of sick leave per year: the first 2 weeks at full
pay, the next 4 weeks at three quarters pay, the remainder unpaid.

End of service. Gratuity is calculated at 15 days basic salary per year for the
first 3 years, and one month per year thereafter.
