COMMERCIAL INSURANCE POLICY — Schedule of Cover

Policy period. 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027.

Property damage. Cover up to 400,000 OMR for the insured premises and contents.
Excess of 500 OMR applies to each claim.

Public liability. Cover up to 1,000,000 OMR for any one occurrence. No excess
applies to public liability claims.

Business interruption. Indemnity period of 12 months, cover up to 250,000 OMR.

Exclusions. This policy does not cover flood damage, wear and tear, or loss
arising from cyber incidents. Cyber cover is available as a separate
endorsement.

Claims. Claims must be notified within 30 days of the insured becoming aware of
the event.
