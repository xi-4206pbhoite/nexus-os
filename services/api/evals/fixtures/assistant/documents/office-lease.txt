OFFICE LEASE — Unit 402, Al Khuwair Business Centre

Term. The lease runs for 3 years from 1 January 2026, with an option to renew
for a further 2 years on the same terms.

Rent. Annual rent is 18,000 OMR, payable quarterly in advance. Rent is subject
to review at the start of the renewal term, capped at a 5 percent increase.

Notice. Either party may terminate at the end of any lease year by giving 6
months written notice.

Service charge. A service charge of 1,200 OMR per year covers cleaning,
security and common area maintenance. Utilities are billed separately and are
the tenant's responsibility.

Deposit. A security deposit of 4,500 OMR is held for the duration of the lease
and refunded within 60 days of vacant possession.
