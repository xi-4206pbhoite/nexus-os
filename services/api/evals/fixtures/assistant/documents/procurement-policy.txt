PROCUREMENT POLICY — Meridian Trading LLC

Approval thresholds. Purchases up to 500 OMR may be approved by a line manager.
Purchases from 500 to 5,000 OMR require department head approval. Purchases
above 5,000 OMR require the approval of two directors.

Competitive quotes. Three written quotations are required for any purchase
above 2,000 OMR. Where three quotations cannot be obtained, a single source
justification must be recorded.

Preferred suppliers. Suppliers on the approved list are reviewed annually.
A supplier may be removed from the list for repeated late delivery.

Conflicts of interest. Any employee with a personal interest in a supplier must
declare it in writing before taking part in a purchasing decision.

Records. Purchase records are retained for 7 years.
