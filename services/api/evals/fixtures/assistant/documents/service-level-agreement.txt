SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT — Managed IT Services

Availability. The supplier guarantees 99.5 percent monthly availability of the
hosted platform, measured over each calendar month excluding scheduled
maintenance.

Scheduled maintenance. Up to 4 hours per month, notified at least 5 working
days in advance, performed between 00:00 and 04:00 Gulf Standard Time.

Response times. Priority 1 incidents are acknowledged within 30 minutes and
resolved within 4 hours. Priority 2 incidents are acknowledged within 2 hours
and resolved within 1 working day.

Service credits. Availability below 99.5 percent in a month entitles the
customer to a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee, rising to 15
percent if availability falls below 98 percent.

Reporting. A service report is issued within 5 working days of each month end.
