SUPPLIER AGREEMENT — Meridian Trading LLC and Qurum Components SAOC

1. Payment terms. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice date. Invoices are
   issued monthly in arrears.
2. Late payment. Late payment attracts interest at 8 percent per annum,
   calculated daily on the outstanding balance.
3. Early settlement. A discount of 2 percent applies to invoices settled within
   10 days of issue.
4. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement with ninety days
   written notice. Termination for material breach is immediate.
5. Liability. Total liability under this agreement is capped at 50,000 OMR per
   incident and 150,000 OMR in aggregate per contract year.
6. Currency. All amounts are stated in Omani Rial unless otherwise specified.
