VEHICLE FLEET POLICY — Meridian Trading LLC

Eligibility. A company vehicle is provided to employees whose role requires
travel on more than 8 days per month.

Fuel. Fuel cards are issued per vehicle. Personal mileage must be recorded and
is recharged at 0.150 OMR per kilometre.

Maintenance. Vehicles are serviced every 10,000 kilometres or every 6 months,
whichever comes first. Servicing is booked through the fleet administrator.

Accidents. All accidents must be reported to the fleet administrator on the day
they occur, regardless of damage. A police report is required for any incident
involving a third party.

Fines. Traffic fines are the responsibility of the driver and are deducted from
salary unless successfully appealed.
