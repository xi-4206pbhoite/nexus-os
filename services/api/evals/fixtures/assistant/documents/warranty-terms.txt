WARRANTY TERMS — Equipment Supply

Warranty period. Equipment is warranted against defects in materials and
workmanship for 24 months from the date of delivery.

Extended warranty. A further 12 months may be purchased within 30 days of
delivery at 4 percent of the equipment value.

Exclusions. The warranty does not cover consumable parts, damage caused by
incorrect installation, or equipment modified without written consent.

Claims. A warranty claim must be accompanied by the original delivery note.
Replacement parts are dispatched within 5 working days of a claim being
accepted.

Onsite attendance. Onsite attendance is included for equipment within 100
kilometres of Muscat. Beyond that distance, travel is charged at cost.
